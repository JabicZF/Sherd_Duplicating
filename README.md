# Sherd_Duplicating
A small Datapack that allows Pottery Sherd Duplication (1.20+)

From 1.26 onwards, Two version of the datapack will be released: One with the "classic" armour-trim duplication style, and one with a simpler, cheaper recipe. Both Datapacks will be released for every Minecraft version, one as the "Classic" release, and one as the "Simple" release.

The Classic Version aims to give a similar feel to Armour Trim Duplication, namely that this is not a cheap process (28 bricks could either be made into 7 blank pots, or 1 decorated pot), and that the item used to duplicate is consumed by the process and imbued into the new Sherd. This has its benefits and drawbacks, namely that Pottery Sherds retain some level of rarity, in line with the vanilla game.

The Simple Version aims to make Sherds a practical resource for builders to create with, and limit the amount of times a builder has to go out searching for more Sherds, especially the rarer ones. This version of the pack intentionally destroys that sense of rarity around Sherds that exists in Vanilla, for the explicit purpose of making them an easier thing to build with.

Both of these versions have their merits, and even I as the creator am not sure which one I prefer, and I have both of them installed on various worlds/servers, each suiting the vibe of that world/server.

## Simple Version Recipe
In the Simple Version, every Sherd can be duplicated by placing it in the crafting grid alongside 1 brick and 1 black dye. This removes the "duplication item" from the Classic Version, and lowers the required bricks from 7 to just 1, which makes sense given that a brick can be substituted for a Sherd in a pot crafting recipe.
